# Where did these words come from?

How fun is it that one gets to live, isn't it?

That one gets to experience life as a series of events, rather than one single whole.

And how difficult it is to realize that this isn't more complex than that – when your ego tells you its you who's suffering, it's you who's getting the wrong side of things.

It's a bummer that many spend more time focused on these shennanigans, but then again, is the way of the universe to have instances of itself discover different parts of the whole. That whole also includes what we call bad omen.

In front of me I have the immensity of the Punilla Valley. Seen from this angle, millions of people's lives are compressed into one panorama. One person's experience of grief, is another person's realization that the kid they were looking for finally has attached to the uterus.

It is all happening in this instant, while I observe from afar. Flattened by the acknowledgement that I too am the observer and the observed.

We came to experience. Full stop.

Reasoning is only good insofar as it helps you predict how to avoid death, such that you can continue experiencing. Reasoning can turn into the wrong weapon to parse reality when used compulsively.

Words can turn into the wrong translation of reality when taken at face value.

Words will never embed reality transparently. There's no 3D map that can ever describe a 4D territory, regardless of how hard you try - yet the poets, and me, we all try. We all try - because you deserve to be aware too. We all deserve to be aware.

... And i will get angry at life for not giving me the piece of cake I always wanted.

Life smiling back at my naiveness. Desires are an illusion, which falls the logical conclusion that I am also one.

And I hear you, and you judge me, and I judge you back. Why? Because I am human. That's what we humans do - was judgement ever really a thing? 

I judge you because I am, and I human because I am - I can't unbe what I am - in other words, I am meant to rediscover and cycle through because that's what it's necessary in the structure of my being. It can't ever be a failure of the universe, because it is by definition, perfect - as in complete, and never as in good. Morality is too lower dimensional to predict truth.

Energy can't ever be destroyed. Information, for instance, can't either. If transformation is the only option, and destruction can't happen, and information is the undelying layer of reality, then perfection is the obvious conclusion.

Every state is a fragment of that perception. My suffering, their judgement, the happiness, and the realization that I am one with the whole are all one single mesh of truth.

What's harder to hold in the ego is the fact that both illusion and reality coexist simultaneously. Illusions may not be the territory, but they are as part of it as the areas we can't ever map. The analogies we use to express dualism are only useful to once in a while remember what one is - but one is this and that simultaneously - therefore is also futile to wish it otherwise.

I am experience, experiencing, the experienced.

And I can't ever be other than the whole, and each of its parts.

Else,

Where are these words coming from?
