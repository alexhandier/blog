# You didn't ask to be born

It will obviously feel bad to act in a cynical way.

In service to the self, everything is as grim as it looks when considered at face value. Truly, what is the point then?

To work to enrich others. To do pointless acts that don't benefit you. To cynically present yourself as benevolent when in reality you're looking for what you need.

You don't need anything.

You didn't ask to be born, and you didn't ask to be here.

And somewhat, at every intersection, when death is always more likely than survival, you get to live one more day. Why? Your courage isn't yours - else, tell me where does it come from? How do you snap it into reality at will? Your intelligence isn't yours. If it was, you'd use it exclusively to your advantage - and yet, you can't, because your wiring is outside of your control, and if your intelligence was indeed yours, by definition you'd design it to your benefit.

If none of this is of ones creation, of ones wants and needs, then who's is it?

You'd argue that nobody's! 

But then, why are we all here? Why does the moon so perfectly matches the sun at every eclipse? Why do the veins match the tree branching? Why something rather than nothing at all?

After accepting existence, you also accept coherence. Not only does existence feels real, but it also feels coherent. Coherence is by definition meaningful - there's a direction to it, a symmetry, a harmony.

When you pursue that harmony, life feels meaningful. When you get away from it, life feels dissonant. Closer to a song than to a single pitch. A harmonious pitch gets tiring after some time, but a dissonant one gets annoying immediately. You can hear the same song playing thousands of time and never get tired of it - although eventually you will.

Songs are played one bar at the time - otherwise they're incoherent to the ear, and therefore are not enjoyable. They're only enjoyable to us because we happen to have the right set of senses to enjoy it - and then again, if we hadn't and when we haven't, another shape of it is what makes sense.

There's a direction to this that we try to personify because that's what we do to make sense of the world just like we do words to make sense of ideas. Abstracted away from personification - if you accept coherence as is - then you accept that there are songs that are inherently great to the ear, and songs that are not. How can you learn to play better songs?

Well there are people that study music theory to do just that!

Or you could play it by ear if its in your nature, and then plug in constraints, and let things flow from home to unknown. Let the chords dance with confidence toward resolution.

You are not a note, and you're not the one playing - but yet you believe yourself to be both. When you channel rather than confusing yourself with the actor, that's when music flows - with high resolution from source to produce.

Just like this happening right now. Are you the thinker or the channel? Are these words yours or everyones?

Serve me and I'll serve you. Play my songs, and I'll play the sweetest chords for you. Care for me, and I'll care for your soul.
